# Privacy Policy – Farm2Pizza

**Developer:** RamTechStudios  
**Application:** Farm2Pizza  
**Effective date:** 22 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

RamTechStudios ("we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy of all players, including children and teenagers.

This Privacy Policy explains how Farm2Pizza handles information when you play our game.

Farm2Pizza is an offline mobile game. We do not collect, request, store or transmit players' personal information to our servers.

## 2. Information Collection

Farm2Pizza does not collect personal information, including:

- Names or email addresses.
- Telephone numbers.
- Location information.
- Contacts or personal files.
- Payment information.
- Personal account information.

The game does not require users to create an account, register or provide personal information to play.

## 3. Children's Privacy

Farm2Pizza is designed to be suitable for children, teenagers and adults.

We take children's privacy seriously.

- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
- We do not require children to provide names, email addresses or other personal details.
- We do not require account creation or registration.
- We do not knowingly track children or build profiles about them.
- We do not intentionally use advertising or analytics services that collect children's personal information.

Children can enjoy the game's core features without submitting personal information.

Parents and guardians can contact us using the details provided in this policy if they have questions about children's privacy.

## 4. Local Game Data

Farm2Pizza uses local storage on the player's device to save game progress and settings.

This may include:

- Game progress.
- In-game currency and upgrades.
- Purchased or unlocked in-game items.
- Game settings and preferences.

This information remains stored locally on the device and is not transmitted to RamTechStudios.

Players or their parents and guardians can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game. Device-level backup settings may also affect how application data is retained.

## 5. Internet Connectivity

Farm2Pizza is designed to be played offline and does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay.

The game does not transmit personal information to external servers.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Farm2Pizza does not intentionally integrate advertising, analytics or tracking services that collect players' personal information.

We do not knowingly share children's personal information with third parties.

If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain any relevant changes to data handling.

## 7. Data Security

Since game progress is stored locally on the player's device and we do not operate a server collecting player information, we do not maintain a central database of player personal information.

Players and their parents or guardians are responsible for the security of their devices and any device backups.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its functionality changes.

Any updated version will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with a revised effective date where appropriate.
