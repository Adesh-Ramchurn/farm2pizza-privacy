# Privacy Policy – Farm2Pizza

**Developer:** RamTechStudios
**Application:** Farm2Pizza
**Effective date:** 17 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

RamTechStudios ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Farm2Pizza handles information when you play our game.

Farm2Pizza is an offline mobile game. We do not collect, request, store or transmit players' personal information to our servers.

## 2. Information Collection

Farm2Pizza does not collect personal information, including:

- Names or email addresses.
- Telephone numbers.
- Location information.
- Contacts or personal files.
- Payment information.
- Personal account information.

The game does not require users to create an account or register to play.

## 3. Local Game Data

Farm2Pizza uses local storage on your device to save game progress and settings.

This may include:

- Game progress.
- In-game currency and upgrades.
- Purchased or unlocked in-game items.
- Game settings and preferences.

This information remains stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to RamTechStudios.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the game. Device-level backup settings may also affect how application data is retained.

## 4. Internet Connectivity

Farm2Pizza is designed to be played offline and does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay.

The game does not transmit personal information to external servers.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Farm2Pizza does not intentionally integrate advertising, analytics or tracking services that collect players' personal information.

## 6. Data Security

Since game progress is stored locally on your device and we do not operate a server collecting player information, we do not maintain a central database of player personal information.

You are responsible for the security of your own device and its backups.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Farm2Pizza does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

As the game does not collect personal information, players can enjoy the game without providing personal details.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its functionality changes.

Any updated version will be published at the same privacy policy URL, with a revised effective date where appropriate.
